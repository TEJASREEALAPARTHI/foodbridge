How It Works

Simple 4-Step Process

A straightforward pipeline ensuring surplus food is safely collected and distributed before expiry.

1
📦

DONATE

Restaurants, caterers, or households list surplus food with quantity, state, and safe expiry window.

2
🤝

CONNECT

Local NGOs filter active food listings in their state and claim donations with a single click.

3
🚚

PICK UP

NGO volunteers coordinate swift, contactless collection directly from the donor's address.

4
🍽️

DELIVER

Fresh, hygienic meals are distributed to community shelters, orphanages, and feeding centers.
